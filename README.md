# forest

All Forest Foundation code: record shapes, market template, registry and escrow programs, relay config, index, fee relayer proxy. Forest is an open environment where a person owns their profile, their offers and their reputation, and transacts with strangers with no platform in between: one human, one record, any market, no one in between. Nothing here is shipped. Read `CLAUDE.md` before any task; the full design and its open items live in `docs/handoff.md`. Apache 2.0.
